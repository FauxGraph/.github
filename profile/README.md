# Welcome to FauxGraph!

## What is FauxGraph?

FauxGraph makes development easy for Azure Resource Graph and Microsoft Graph APIs, providing:

- Faster integration - skip the headache hurdles and begin developing immediately.
- Improved product quality - incorporate FauxGraph in continuous integration and developers' inner loops.
- Validation - broader data sets help uncover the unknown unknowns in your product.

## Why FauxGraph?

FauxGraph was born out of necessity. While planning a product for AzGraph, we recognized the lack of support for developing and testing products in this space. We decided to open FauxGraph for others to use, rather than build our own, private environment and set of data.

Visit [FauxGraph.com](https://FauxGraph.com) to get started!
